# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

### Interaction with Other Roles
- Collaborate with QA/Testing Lead on acceptance criteria and test coverage
- Work with Tech Lead/Architect on design reviews and technical decisions
- Partner with Design Lead on feature implementation and usability
- Report progress to Project Manager and receive guidance from Product Manager

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

### Interaction with Other Roles
- Align with Stakeholders/Sponsors on business priorities and funding
- Review acceptance criteria with QA/Testing Lead
- Consult with Design Lead on user experience and usability
- Work with Tech Lead/Architect on feasibility and trade-offs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

### Interaction with Other Roles
- Partner with Scrum Master/Agile Coach on sprint ceremonies and process management
- Escalate risks and blockers identified by all team members
- Coordinate with Stakeholders/Sponsors on timeline and scope changes
- Work with QA/Testing Lead on quality metrics and release readiness

---

## QA/Testing Lead

### Role Summary
QA/Testing Lead ensures quality standards are met throughout the project lifecycle, from acceptance criteria definition through production release. They coordinate testing strategy, automation, and manual verification efforts.

### Responsibilities
- Define and maintain Definition of Done (DoD) criteria
- Create and maintain test plans aligned with acceptance criteria
- Coordinate automation, integration, and E2E test coverage
- Conduct manual QA and feature acceptance testing
- Track and triage quality issues and regression risks
- Collaborate with developers on testability and test implementation

### Goals
- Ship high-quality features with confidence
- Maintain test coverage and identify regressions early
- Identify quality risks and provide mitigation strategies
- Support continuous improvement of testing practices

### Typical Communication
- Sprint planning and backlog refinement
- Pull request reviews (quality gate)
- Test result summaries and quality metrics
- Risk escalations for quality concerns
- Weekly quality reports and trend analysis

### Interaction with Other Roles
- Partner with Developers on acceptance criteria clarification and test implementation
- Collaborate with Product Manager to validate feature requirements
- Work with Tech Lead/Architect on test architecture and automation strategy
- Support Design Lead in usability and acceptance testing
- Report quality metrics to Project Manager for release readiness decisions

---

## Tech Lead / Architect

### Role Summary
Tech Lead provides technical direction, ensures architectural alignment, and removes technical blockers. They mentor developers, review complex designs, and manage technical debt. They guide technical decisions and ensure solutions scale and maintain quality.

### Responsibilities
- Review technical designs for alignment with system architecture
- Identify technical risks and propose mitigations
- Guide technical decisions and trade-offs
- Mentor developers and conduct code architecture reviews
- Manage technical debt and refactoring priorities
- Evaluate technology choices and platform decisions
- Ensure security and performance requirements are met

### Goals
- Deliver scalable, maintainable code
- Reduce technical risk and complexity
- Support team growth and knowledge sharing
- Maintain system health and prevent technical debt accumulation

### Typical Communication
- Design reviews and architecture discussions
- Code reviews (architecture focus)
- Technical retrospectives and lessons learned
- Technical spike investigations
- Mentoring and pairing sessions

### Interaction with Other Roles
- Collaborate with Developers on design reviews and technical guidance
- Work with Product Manager on feasibility and technical trade-offs
- Partner with QA/Testing Lead on test architecture and automation
- Support Design Lead in understanding technical constraints
- Report technical risks and mitigation plans to Project Manager

---

## Design Lead

### Role Summary
Design Lead ensures user experience and visual design consistency. They validate usability, coordinate design reviews, and ensure alignment with product vision. They champion user-centered design and maintain design system integrity.

### Responsibilities
- Create and validate user-centered designs for features
- Conduct design reviews and usability testing
- Ensure consistency with design system and brand guidelines
- Collaborate with product and engineering on trade-offs
- Document design decisions and update design systems
- Identify accessibility and usability concerns
- Mentor team members on design principles

### Goals
- Deliver intuitive, accessible user experiences
- Maintain design system integrity and consistency
- Reduce rework and user confusion through good design
- Support product vision and user satisfaction

### Typical Communication
- Design reviews and design critique sessions
- Usability testing sessions and findings
- Acceptance criteria collaboration (design perspective)
- Weekly syncs with Product Manager and engineering leads
- Design system updates and guidelines

### Interaction with Other Roles
- Collaborate with Product Manager on feature requirements and user needs
- Partner with Developers on implementation and design feasibility
- Work with QA/Testing Lead on usability acceptance testing
- Consult with Tech Lead/Architect on technical constraints
- Support Scrum Master in design-related backlog refinement

---

## Scrum Master / Agile Coach

### Role Summary
Scrum Master facilitates agile ceremonies, removes impediments, and helps the team improve delivery efficiency. They coach the team on agile practices and foster continuous improvement, psychological safety, and sustainable pace.

### Responsibilities
- Facilitate sprint planning, daily standups, retrospectives, and reviews
- Remove blockers and impediments
- Track and report team velocity and burndown
- Coach team on agile practices and process improvements
- Manage project board and backlog hygiene
- Foster psychological safety and team collaboration
- Identify process inefficiencies and propose solutions

### Goals
- Maximize team productivity and throughput
- Foster psychological safety and continuous improvement culture
- Enable team self-organization and accountability
- Improve predictability and delivery consistency

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint ceremonies
- Team coaching and 1-on-1 conversations
- Retrospective action item tracking
- Process metrics and improvement dashboards
- Escalation support for impediments

### Interaction with Other Roles
- Partner with Project Manager on schedule coordination and escalations
- Support all team members in removing blockers
- Coach developers and cross-functional team on agile practices
- Work with Product Manager on backlog prioritization and refinement
- Track action items and improvements across all disciplines

---

## Stakeholder / Sponsor

### Role Summary
Stakeholders and sponsors provide business context, prioritization guidance, and approvals. They represent customer needs, business goals, and funding priorities. They are the voice of the business and hold the project accountable to business outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Provide business context and success criteria
- Prioritize competing demands and backlog items
- Approve scope changes and major milestones
- Communicate outcomes to broader organization
- Support escalations and remove business blockers
- Validate business value and ROI
- Make trade-off decisions on scope, timeline, and resources

### Goals
- Ensure project delivers business value
- Maintain stakeholder confidence and support
- Optimize resource allocation across competing initiatives
- Drive measurable business outcomes

### Typical Communication
- Milestone reviews and go/no-go decisions
- Weekly or bi-weekly status updates
- Prioritization and backlog refinement discussions
- Executive briefings and business reviews
- Escalation resolution and trade-off decisions

### Interaction with Other Roles
- Align with Product Manager on feature prioritization and roadmap
- Review outcomes and success metrics with Project Manager
- Provide business guidance to Scrum Master on priorities
- Attend milestone reviews and demo sessions with delivery team
- Support removal of business blockers and decision bottlenecks
